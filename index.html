Event-first: the Slack is where we coordinate around tech events — what’s happening this week, who’s heading out, and side conversations after. Engineers, product folks, founders and operators mostly in Paris and Berlin, with channels for Munich and Hamburg too.

Why people join

  • Stay on top of tech meetups, talks and community events — curated, not a firehose
